# AX-HUD 1.2.2

The layout editor strip hides and reveals.

## Changed

* An **arrow at the right end of the strip** folds it to that one small button, and unfolds it again. The choice is remembered, and the
  back key still finishes editing.
* The strip **steps aside by itself** while you drag or resize a card, and comes back when you let go.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
