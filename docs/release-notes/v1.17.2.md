# AX-HUD 1.17.2

## New

* **Fit the hills to the flight.** The log height and the elevation data differ by some metres, so the ribbon could dip under the ground in the 3D replay. The aircraft cannot be under the ground, so the hills are now lowered (by up to 60 m, never raised) until the lowest point of the flight is a metre above them. Settings > Display > Fit the hills to the flight turns it off.

## Notes

* It corrects one constant shift for the whole flight. Local errors of the elevation data (trees, buildings, a 15 to 20 m grid) remain.
