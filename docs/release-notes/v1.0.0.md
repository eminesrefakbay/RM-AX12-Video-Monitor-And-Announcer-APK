# AX-HUD 1.0.0

The first stable release of AX-HUD: a video monitor, HUD and voice announcer for the RadioMaster AX12.

## Install

Download **`ax-hud.apk`**, install it on the AX12 (allow installs from unknown sources) and open it. Allow the camera permission: the
HDMI input reaches Android as a camera. A short welcome guide explains the first steps. `SHA256SUMS` lets you check the download.
`ax-hud-plain.apk` is the same app without the serial-lock patch and is for tests only.

## What it does

* Full-screen video from the AX12's HDMI input, a gray "No video" screen and automatic reconnecting.
* A HUD over the video: live charts, a radar map, flight statistics, a stick view with channel bars, a warning bar with three levels
  and a pre-flight check that says READY.
* Spoken announcements from rules you write, plus battery per cell, link quality, telemetry loss and the flight mode.
* Aircraft and battery profiles (Li-ion and LiPo), drag-and-drop layout, backup of all settings.
* English, Turkish, German and Simplified Chinese.
* It only reads from the radio. It never writes to it.

## New since 0.15

* A **welcome guide** on the first start (and under Settings > About).
* The update channel is **Stable** by default.
* Licensed under the **GPL v3 or later**, with an additional permission for the RadioMaster AX SDK.
* A Chinese screen language is skipped on a device without a Chinese font; texts were checked for fit in all four languages.

## Known limits

* The radio data appears about **12 seconds** after you open the app. That wait is inside the RadioMaster SDK, and a banner counts it.
* Tested on one setup only: AX12 (Android 9), ExpressLRS and INAV 9.1. Other radios, receivers and flight controllers are untested.
* German and Chinese texts are machine-assisted and not reviewed by native speakers yet.
* AX-HUD is an aid, not a safety system. Do not rely on it as your only warning, and fly within your local rules.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
