# AX-HUD 1.17.3

## New

* **Ribbon off.** The trail button in the 3D replay has a third choice, Off: no ribbon and no colour legend, for a clean view.
* **FPV camera angle.** Settings > Display > FPV camera angle (default 25 degrees), or drag up or down in the FPV view; the angle shows for a moment and is remembered. It applies to a quad; a plane looks along its nose.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
