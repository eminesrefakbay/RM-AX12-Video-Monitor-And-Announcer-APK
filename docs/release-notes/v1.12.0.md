# AX-HUD 1.12.0

## New

* **Settings are tabs, with a search.** A rail on the left has eight tabs: General, Aircraft and flight, Video and overlays, Dashboard,
  Antenna tracker, Flight logs, Rules and backup and System. The rows of the open tab are on the right. The 14 collapsible groups of earlier
  versions are folded into the tabs: the approval window and the landing, link loss and packs rows are headings in Aircraft and flight, and
  the two groups that were both called Diagnostics are one section of System.
* **A search field in the title bar** finds a setting in every tab by its words. Capitals and accents do not matter, every word must be in
  the label, and the results are the real rows under their tab and heading, so a switch or a value works right in the results. Tapping a tab
  clears the search.
* The tab names and the search texts are in English, Turkish, German and Chinese.

The rows and their behaviour are unchanged. This is the biggest change to Settings so far and it has not been seen on a radio yet: please tell
me if a tab feels cramped, a row is missing, or the keyboard misbehaves.
