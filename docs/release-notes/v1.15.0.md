# AX-HUD 1.15.0

## New

* **Return reserve.** A card on the HUD compares what is left in the pack with what the flight home would take, and calls out before the margin is gone.
  Speed, current, extra distance and the landing reserve are kept for each pack. The values reserve_pct and reserve_s can be used in rules, charts and logs.
* **Manoeuvres in the replay.** Dives, rolls and flips are found from the log, marked on the scrub bar and listed; a tap jumps there. A dive has a report
  with start and bottom height, angle, top speed, fall time, recovery and peak current.
* **Flight summary** for every log: time, highest point, farthest distance, speeds, battery used, lowest cell, peak current and the approval window.
* **Export** as GPX, KML, CSV (every value or the main ones) or report text, saved to the radio or copied. The GPS positions can be taken out.
* **Backup and restore** with a choice of what goes in, the date of the last backup and a reminder after 30 days.
* **A layout for each aircraft,** with a switch to turn it off.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
