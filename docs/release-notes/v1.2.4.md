# AX-HUD 1.2.4

No more editing by accident.

## Changed

* **Hold one spot for 5 seconds to edit the layout**, on the dashboard and on the video. In 1.2.3 a short long press opened the editor, and
  a slow swipe could trigger it. Now a swipe, a drag or any movement of the finger (more than about 4 mm) cancels the hold, and so does a
  second finger or letting go.
* On the video the hold is still ignored while the aircraft is armed, over a panel and on the MENU button, and it is checked again when
  the 5 seconds are over.
* The tick and the back key still return straight to the page you were on.
