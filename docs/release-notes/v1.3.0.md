# AX-HUD 1.3.0

Around the flight.

## New

* **Last known position.** When the link is lost in flight, a card shows the last position with its distance and bearing from home, the
  voice says it, and it is saved on the radio (and refreshed every 5 seconds in flight). Copy it or open it in a maps app from
  *Settings > Landing, link loss and packs > Last known position*.
* **Approval window.** Set the time you are approved to fly. A chip shows the time left, the voice warns at the minutes you choose while the
  aircraft is armed, speaks the end, and warns if you arm outside the window. It repeats daily and may cross midnight.
* **Distance, energy and efficiency.** Added up for every flight, kept after landing, available as values (`flown_km`, `energy_wh`, `wh_km`,
  `mah_km`, `win_left`) in value cards, charts, rules and logs, and shown in the flight list (also for old logs).
* **Pack book.** Totals per pack and its last ten flights, counted when you land: *Settings > Landing, link loss and packs > Packs*. Part of
  the settings backup.
* **Voice debrief after landing:** time, distance, capacity, efficiency, lowest cell, the voltage the pack rested at, and the flight number
  of the pack.

## Changed

* The build pins the SDK to one commit instead of taking the newest, so a change on their side cannot break or alter a release.
* German, Turkish and Chinese texts for all of the above (machine-assisted; corrections are welcome).
