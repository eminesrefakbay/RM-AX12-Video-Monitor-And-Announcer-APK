# AX-HUD 1.13.0

## New: 3D flight replay (first version)

Open a flight log and the replay now shows the flight in 3D behind your cards: a speed-coloured ribbon over a flat ground with a horizon and a grid,
the aircraft on it, a green HOME ring, and flags at the events (take-off, every flight-mode change, the end of the approval window, the farthest
point, the highest point, the top speed, landing).

* **Smooth motion.** The log holds a position and an attitude about once a second (the telemetry rate of a long-range link). The aircraft moves on a
  smooth curve through those points (a cubic Hermite curve for the position, a quaternion squad curve for the attitude), so there are no corners and no
  steps, at any screen frame rate. The 3D view runs its own clock on every screen frame and only eases towards the replay's position.
* **Three cameras.** One button on the replay bar: Chase (behind and above, follows with a half-life), Orbit (drag to turn, pinch to zoom) and Pilot
  (fixed at home, the lens closes in as the aircraft goes away). A seek puts the camera at the new place at once.
* The cards, the charts and the scrub bar of the replay are the same as before, and they sit over the 3D view. A log without a position shows a short
  note instead.

Known limits of this first version: the picture is drawn with the canvas, not yet with OpenGL; the timeline has no event markers yet; the signs of
pitch and roll are assumed (see `PITCH_SIGN` and `ROLL_SIGN` in `FlightPath.java` if the aircraft leans the wrong way).
