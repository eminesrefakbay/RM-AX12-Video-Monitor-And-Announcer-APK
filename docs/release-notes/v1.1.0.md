# AX-HUD 1.1.0

Flight logs and replay.

## New

* **Flight logs** (Settings > Flight logs, off by default): a CSV file for every flight, from arming to disarming. You choose the values
  (every channel and telemetry value the radio publishes), the rate (1 to 25 rows a second) and how many flights to keep.
  The files are on the radio under `Android/data/dev.ax12.announcer/files/flights/` and open in any spreadsheet.
* **Flight list and replay**: each flight with its summary (date, aircraft, length, pack, highest point, farthest distance, top speed,
  peak current, lowest cell, mAh used). Replay draws the statistics, radar with its track, stick view, channel bars and charts from the
  log, with play, pause, 1x to 8x and a scrub bar.
* The welcome guide mentions the flight logs.

## Safe by design

The log is written on its own thread: slow or failing storage can never hold up the radio link or the screen. Dropped rows are counted,
a full storage stops the log and says so, and a log that was cut off is still readable.

## Privacy

A log contains GPS positions and stays on the radio. Do not publish one that shows where you fly.

## Known limits

* Replay has no video (the video is not recorded) and no voice.
* A card in a replay shows only what the log holds; the Replay set holds everything a replay needs.
* Not yet checked on a radio by native speakers: German and Chinese texts. The radio data still appears about 12 seconds after you open the app
  (inside the RadioMaster SDK).
