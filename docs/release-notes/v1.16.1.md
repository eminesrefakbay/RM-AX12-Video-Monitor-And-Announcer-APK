# AX-HUD 1.16.1

## New

* **Street, terrain or satellite under the 3D replay.** The Map button in the replay opens a menu: Off, Street map (OpenStreetMap), Satellite (Esri World Imagery) and Terrain (OpenTopoMap, with contour lines and shading). Settings > Display has the map type, and a custom tile address of your own as a fourth kind. Each kind has its own cache.
* **Why there is no map.** While no tile is on the screen, the corner of the view says that the map is loading, or why the tiles could not be had (no network, or the answer of the server).

## Notes

* The terrain and satellite maps stop at zoom 17: a very small flight area is drawn a little coarser than with the street map.
* The built-in map types use public tile servers without a key. Check the terms of the server you use (Settings > Display), and use the custom address for a provider you have an account with. Each server sees which area you look at.
