# AX-HUD 1.11.0

## New: antenna tracker support

* **A Bluetooth LE link from the radio to an antenna tracker** (an ESP32 with two servos, a GPS and a compass). The radio sends the aircraft's
  position, speed, course and height above the launch point five times a second; the tracker does all the pointing itself, because it knows
  its own position (GPS) and the direction its base faces (compass).
* **Settings > Antenna tracker:** use the tracker, search for it once (a chosen tracker is connected by its address), follow the aircraft or
  hold, centre the antennas, a 30-second test sweep, compass calibration, the magnetic declination and the antenna height above the launch
  point. The status line shows the connection, the tracker's GPS, the compass and whether it follows.
* **The firmware and the build instructions are in the `tracker/` folder** (PlatformIO, ESP32 Arduino). The geometry (distance, bearing,
  elevation, dead reckoning, the flip of the tilt servo that lets two 180-degree servos cover every direction, slew limiting) and the packets
  are plain C++, tested on a PC against the same packets as the app's tests (52 checks in C++, 98 in the app's Java tests).
* Android asks for the location permission only to search for the tracker, once; AX-HUD does not read your location. The app never asks for
  it in flight, and keeps the radio link while the dialog is open.

## Not yet tested on hardware

The Bluetooth, servo, GPS and compass parts of the firmware and the app's Bluetooth link have been written and checked on a PC (syntax and the
tested core), but not yet run on an ESP32 and a radio. Please report what you see in *Settings > Diagnostics* (events named "tracker").

German, Turkish and Chinese texts are included (machine-assisted).
