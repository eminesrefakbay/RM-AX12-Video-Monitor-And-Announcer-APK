# AX-HUD 1.13.3

## Changed

* **The aircraft in the 3D replay is easier to read.** A flight log shows that the yaw and the model's nose agree with the direction of flight (in your
  12 Nov log the nose points along the motion in 95% of the fast moments), but in a fast run the aircraft is pitched 30 degrees nose down, and from
  a camera behind it that can look like a plane flying tail first. Now: an orange arrow at the aircraft's height points along its heading, a
  triangle on the ground does the same, the plane is drawn with filled shapes (wing, tail plane, fin, orange nose cone), both models are a little
  bigger, and the chase camera is a little higher.
