# AX-HUD 1.14.0

## New

* **A video-player bar for the 3D replay.** The scrub bar runs over the whole width at the bottom, with a mark for every event of the flight (take-off,
  mode changes, the highest point, landing). Under it: Stop, previous and next event, 10 seconds back and forward, Play or Pause, the time, the speed
  (0.5x, 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x) and Loop. Dragging the bar scrubs, a tap jumps.
* **A rail of buttons on the right.** Close, the camera (Chase, Orbit, Pilot and a new Top view), the aircraft (quad or plane), the colour of the trail
  (by speed or by height), Cards (hides the cards so the 3D view can be seen alone) and Snapshot (a picture of the screen without the bar).
* The bar and the rail fade out after four seconds and come back with a touch.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
