# AX-HUD 1.15.4

## Fixed

* **Replay buttons and the player bar.** The buttons on the right no longer overlap the bar at the bottom. They measure the room above it and use two columns when
  one column does not fit. The moves list follows the same room.
* **The red REPLAY tag** at the top now fades out with the bar and the buttons, so it no longer covers the cards underneath.

## Changed

* **No still logo screen at start.** The logo that Android showed before the animated splash is gone; the app opens on plain navy and goes straight into the splash.
