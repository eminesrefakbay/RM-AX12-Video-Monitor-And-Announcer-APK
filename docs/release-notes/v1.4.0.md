# AX-HUD 1.4.0

Readable in direct sun.

## New

* **Sunlight theme.** Dark text on light, nearly solid backgrounds for the **MENU, Settings, the rules, About, the flight logs, the packs and
  position pages, the welcome guide, the dialogs and the dashboard page** (with all its cards). It reads better in direct sun than light on
  dark, where the glare lifts the black of the screen.
* **A Sunlight tile in the MENU** switches it at once; the same switch is in *Settings > Display*. The choice is kept and is part of the
  settings backup. The MENU now has nine tiles (five in the first row).
* **Stronger warnings** in the theme: a saturated red for critical, a bright yellow with black text for a warning, a deep blue for
  information, with a thick outline, bigger bold text and thicker marks.

## Not changed

* The cards over the video, the banners and the layout editor keep their look.
* The dark look is still the default.

German, Turkish and Chinese texts for the new switch and the guide are included (machine-assisted; corrections are welcome).
