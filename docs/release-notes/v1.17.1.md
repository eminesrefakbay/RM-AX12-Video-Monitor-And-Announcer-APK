# AX-HUD 1.17.1

## New

* **FPV camera in the 3D replay.** The camera button has a fifth view: the eye sits on the aircraft and looks along its nose, tilted up 25 degrees for a quad (none for a plane), and the horizon turns with the roll. The aircraft itself is not drawn.
* Satellite pictures on the hills: choose Satellite as the map type and leave Hills in 3D on (Settings > Display).

## Notes

* The roll and pitch come from the log. If the horizon turns the wrong way, tell me and the sign can be flipped.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
