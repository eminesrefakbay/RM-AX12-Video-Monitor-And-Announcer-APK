# AX-HUD 1.2.0

A dashboard page, with a horizon and a compass.

## New

* **Dashboard**: a second page with cards only and no video (the camera rests), for pilots in goggles who do not use the HDMI input, and for
  a second pair of eyes. Switch pages with the **Dashboard / Video** tile in the MENU; the page you left is the page the app starts on.
* **Horizon**: an artificial horizon from the pitch and roll angles of the flight controller. Settings > Dashboard has switches to invert
  pitch or roll if your flight controller counts the other way.
* **Compass**: a rose that turns with the heading, the heading in degrees, and a marker that points the way home with the distance.
* **Value cards**: any value, with a label, a unit, decimals and amber and red limits (a battery gets worse downwards, a current upwards).
* The statistics, radar, charts and sticks are also available on the dashboard, and a replay of a flight log runs on it too.
* **Edit the layout** with the same editor as the video cards, with Add, Remove and Edit buttons for cards. Part of the settings backup.
* The MENU has eight tiles now: Dashboard (or Video) and Flight logs are new.

## Known limits

* The horizon shows angles, not gyro rates: raw gyro data is not sent in telemetry. Check the direction of pitch and roll on the bench
  (nose down should show more ground) and use the switches in Settings > Dashboard if needed.
* Telemetry attitude arrives a few times a second, so the horizon is smoothed a little and is not a replacement for an FPV view.
* The radio data still appears about 12 seconds after you open the app (inside the RadioMaster SDK).
* German and Chinese texts are machine-assisted and not reviewed by native speakers yet.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
