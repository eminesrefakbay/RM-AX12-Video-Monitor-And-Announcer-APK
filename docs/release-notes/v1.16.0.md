# AX-HUD 1.16.0

## New

* **A map under the 3D replay.** The ground of the replay is now a map: OpenStreetMap tiles by default, or the address of your own tile server (Settings > Display > Map tiles from), laid on the ground in perspective under the ribbon. The tiles that cover a flight (up to 144) are fetched once when you open its replay, two connections at most, and kept in the app's cache (trimmed at 120 MB), so the map also shows at the field without a network.
* **A Map button** on the right side of the replay and a **Map under the 3D replay** switch in Settings. Without a map the grid is drawn as before.
* The credit of the map shows at the bottom left, as the map's licence asks.

## Privacy

The app now asks a tile server for map tiles, which tells that server your address and the area of the flight. Switch the map off and nothing is requested. See the Privacy section of the README.

## Notes

* The ground is flat: hills are on the map but not drawn as hills.
* A key in a tile address is saved in the settings backup. Check the terms of the tile server you use.
