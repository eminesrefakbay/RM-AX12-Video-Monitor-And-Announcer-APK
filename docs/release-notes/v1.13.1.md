# AX-HUD 1.13.1

## Changed

* **The replay bar hides itself.** The bar along the bottom of a replay (Close, Play, speed, camera, model, scrub bar) fades out after 4 seconds
  without a touch, so it no longer covers the sticks and the other cards at the bottom. Touch the screen anywhere and it comes back; it stays while
  you drag the scrub bar.
* **A model for the 3D replay: Quad or Plane.** A new button on the replay bar switches the aircraft that is drawn (a plane has a fuselage, a
  straight wing with a red left and a green right tip, a tail plane and a fin). The choice is remembered.
