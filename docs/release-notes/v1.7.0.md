# AX-HUD 1.7.0

Shapes.

## New

* **Shape styles** for cards, tiles, rows and the warning bar, chosen in *Settings > Display > Shape*: **two cut corners (the default)**, four
  cut corners, corner brackets, soft corners, or plain rectangles as before. The choice is kept and is part of the settings backup.
* It applies to the MENU, Settings, the dashboard and video cards (statistics, radar, charts, checklist, compass, horizon, value cards), the
  banners and the warning bar. Warnings keep a solid fill; with brackets selected they are cut at two corners instead.
* The stick boxes, switches and large text pages stay plain.

German, Turkish and Chinese texts are included (machine-assisted).

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
