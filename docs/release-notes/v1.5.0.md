# AX-HUD 1.5.0

Easier to report a problem.

## New

* **Diagnostics and bug report** (*Settings > Diagnostics*): a page that collects the versions, the device, the state of the radio link with
  its start-up timeline, the state of flight logging, a digest of the settings and the last events of the session. After an unexpected stop
  it also holds the stack trace of the crash.
* **Copy, share or save the report**, add a note about what happened, and open the issue page. *Read the report* shows exactly what would be
  sent.
* **Private by design:** the report holds no GPS positions, no aircraft or pack names, no rules and no layouts, and a filter also takes out
  position pairs, addresses, e-mail addresses and long numbers, even from your own note. Nothing is sent by the app.
* The events cover the app and the link starting, the first configuration arriving, arming and disarming, link loss and recovery, a
  logger problem and update checks that fail.

German, Turkish and Chinese texts are included (machine-assisted; corrections are welcome).

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
