# AX-HUD 1.2.3

Faster layout editing, on both pages.

## Changed

* **Press and hold to edit**: on the **dashboard**, and on the **video**. Settings still has the same entries.
  On the video, a long press is ignored while the aircraft is armed (nothing may pop up over the picture in flight), over a panel, and on
  the MENU button.
* **The tick returns straight to the page you were on**, whichever way editing was started. It no longer opens Settings. The back key does
  the same.

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
