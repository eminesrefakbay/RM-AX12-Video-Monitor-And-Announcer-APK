# AX-HUD 1.8.0

## New

* **An animated splash screen** (about 2.9 seconds, a tap skips it): the frame around the logo draws itself, the logo grows in under a
  passing light, the title appears letter by letter and catches a light, a line draws under it, the tagline follows and the version chip
  draws itself last.
* **Chinese can be chosen on every radio.** The AX12 has no Chinese system font, so the language switch used to skip Chinese after German.
  The app now carries Noto Sans SC (SIL OFL), cut down to the characters its Chinese texts use (two weights, 207 KB each).
* **Buttons and tappable rows follow the shape style.** The command buttons of the sound and warning rules, the buttons and rows inside
  Settings and the buttons in dialogs' content are no longer plain squares.
* Toasts (the short messages at the bottom) use the app's fonts too, so Chinese shows in them.

German, Turkish and Chinese texts for the new splash lines and the credits are included (machine-assisted).

AX-HUD is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by RadioMaster.
